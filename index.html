How I work

A brief warning before hiring me

I ask a lot of questions. Vague goals and fuzzy thinking tend to hide the real problem, so I’ll want to understand the why, the assumptions, and what success should look like.

I care about outcomes more than appearances. I’ll challenge busywork and processes that no longer help, and I’d rather build something people can rely on than polish something that only looks good in a presentation.

I’m direct, but not unkind. When I take something on, I feel responsible for seeing it through: questioning, refining, and working with people until the result is right.

If you want someone curious, analytical, and comfortable taking ownership, we’ll probably work well together.
